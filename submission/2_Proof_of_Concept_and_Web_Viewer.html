1. Executive Summary & Capabilities

Raasta-3D is a client-side, zero-install WebGL application engineered using React 19, Three.js, and GaussianSplats3D. It delivers a fluid 60 FPS first-person interactive walkthrough of indoor spaces, real-time spatial room zone inspection, landmark Point of Interest (POI) searching, and optimal A* pathfinding that strictly avoids real physical obstacles like walls, sofas, and kitchen islands.

2. Quickstart Guide for Evaluators & Judges

  1. Open the URL: Open https://raasta-3d.vercel.app in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari.
  2. Enter Walk Mode: Click the "Walk" button in the top navigation bar, then click anywhere on the 3D canvas to lock mouse pointer look.
  3. Freely Roam the Space: Use W A S D to walk smoothly in first person. Press Shift to sprint and C to crouch. Observe that continuous collision detection stops your avatar from clipping through walls or furniture.
  4. Inspect Room Zones: Click directly on any room floor (Living Room, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, Workstation Study) to open the Room Details Card with designated name, square footage, and key amenities.
  5. Search Specific Objects / POIs: Press Ctrl + K or click the search bar to find landmarks (e.g. "3-Seater Sectional Sofa", "Chef Prep Island").
  6. Request Route Guidance: Click "Navigate Here" on any POI card. An animated glowing cyan ribbon appears on the floor, routing cleanly around obstacles to your target.
  7. Roam to Target: Click the "Roam to Target" button in the top Navigation HUD. The camera auto-walks smoothly along the path all the way to your destination.

4. Core Functional Modules Demonstrated

4.1 Spatial POI & Room Zone Labeling

  • Room Details Card: Clicking on any room zone floor immediately presents its room title, area in square feet, architectural level, and key furniture objects.
  • 3D Glowing Diamond Beacons: Key points of interest are anchored with floating animated octahedrons and pulsing ground radar rings.
  • Name Object / SOP Checkpoint: Tapping any furniture or object opens an instant SOP naming card, enabling operators to label procedural inspection points.

4.2 Optimal Non-Colliding Navigation & Anti-Zigzag Routing

  • Direct Line-of-Sight Routing: If the destination is in the same room or open corridor, the path connects straight to the target without arbitrary detours.
  • Funnel String-Pulling Algorithm: Collapses orthogonal waypoint zigzags into smooth, direct lines around obstacle clearance zones.
  • Doorway Corner Bevels: Rounds corner transitions around doorways so avatar navigation mimics natural human walking trajectories.
  • Top Navigation HUD: Real-time distance countdown, estimated walking time at 1.3 m/s, turn instruction icons (Straight, Turn Left, Turn Right, Arrived).
  • 2D Dynamic Radar Minimap: Real-time floorplan with player location dot, field-of-view vision cone, and active path overlay.

4.3 Client-Side Video-to-3D Grinding Pipeline

  • Users can click "Video to 3D" on the navbar, drop any room video capture, and watch the client-side pipeline extract temporal keyframes, analyze color palettes, recognize interconnected rooms, synthesize 3D Gaussian Splats, and build an interactive digital twin on the fly.
