2. FREE-ROAMING & NAVIGATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR EVALUATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the web link in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
   No plugins or native installations are required.

2. Exploration Modes:
   - Walk Mode (First-Person Free Roam):
     * Click the canvas to lock mouse look.
     * W / A / S / D keys to walk forward, backward, left, right.
     * SHIFT to sprint.
     * C to crouch.
     * ESC to unlock mouse pointer.
   - Orbit Mode:
     * Left click and drag to orbit around the space.
     * Scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
     * Right click and drag to pan.
   - AR Mode:
     * First-person mobile AR walkthrough simulation with dynamic 360° compass
       ribbon, center targeting reticle, and 6-DoF SLAM tracking telemetry.

3. Spatial POI Labeling & Room Inspection:
   - Click directly on any room or zone floor (e.g., Living Room, Kitchen,
     Bedroom, Study) to reveal its designated name, area, and key furniture.
   - Click any 3D glowing diamond beacon to inspect the Point of Interest.
   - Press [Ctrl + K] or click "Search POI" to search by keyword or category.
   - Click "Navigate Here" to see the active optimal path calculated in real-time.

4. Optimal Desktop Navigation:
   - The engine automatically routes an animated glowing 3D ribbon along the
     floor, avoiding physical furniture obstacles (3-seater sofa, tables, beds).
   - The top Navigation HUD displays real-time turn instructions (e.g., "In 4m,
     turn left") and estimated walking time.
   - The bottom-right 2D radar minimap displays player location, heading cone,
     and the route.

5. Loading Custom Splat Models:
   - Click "Load Splat" on the top navbar.
   - Drag and drop any custom .ply, .splat, or .ksplat file into the viewer.
